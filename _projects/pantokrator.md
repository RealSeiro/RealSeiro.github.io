## Overview

From 2022 to 2024 I studied offensive security seriously: binary exploitation, web vulnerabilities, and reverse engineering. I wrote it all up as I went, and the result is **Pantokrator**, a study book anyone can read.

## Results along the way

- **2024 Red Raccoon Hacking Competition**: Excellence Award
- **CCE 2024 (Cyber Conflict Exercise)**: Finalist, Public Sector division

## Why it still matters to me

Security trained me to read systems closely and to assume my first mental model is wrong until I've tested it. I bring the same habit to AI research.

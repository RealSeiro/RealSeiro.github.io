## Overview

Many older adults live alone, and their families can't call every day. This app has two modes: an **elderly mode**, where an AI voice holds a short daily check-in conversation, and a **guardian mode**, where family members see how those check-ins went.

## My role

Three-person team. I owned the server and the database: how check-in records are stored, and how they reach the guardian's side of the app.

## The task

QuantiPhy is a benchmark from Stanford that asks a model to watch a video and answer with a real physical quantity in real units: how big an object is, how fast it moves, how quickly it speeds up. Vision-language models are good at describing what is in a video but often weak at measuring it.

## What I did

I competed as part of a team, worked through the paper and the validation set, and built and tested answer pipelines against the leaderboard.

I'm Dohyun Kim, an undergraduate in the AI department at Gachon University.

I'm interested in how models learn from experience — why today's reinforcement learning signals are so thin, and how an AI system could get better at doing AI research itself. I'm also drawn to alignment: keeping what AI systems value close to what people value.

Before AI, I spent a few years on offensive security (pwnable, web hacking, reversing) and wrote up what I learned in [Pantokrator](https://blackbook.gitbook.io/pantokrator).

This blog is where I write down what I'm reading, testing, and getting wrong.
